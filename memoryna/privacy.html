Privacy Policy

Fabled Oath: Second Dawn · Effective date: 2026-10-11 · Last updated: 2026-10-11

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Fabled Oath: Second Dawn (the "Game"), how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. The Game is made by YCrew Games and operated by Dust In Peace LLC, a company in the United States ("we", "us", "our").

Questions? Email augmentalvision@gmail.com.

1. Information we collect

Collected automatically

  • Player ID — an anonymous player ID created by Unity Authentication the first time you play (a "guest" account).
  • Game progress — your heroes, relics, gear, currencies, story progress, settings and purchase history, stored with Unity Cloud Save and checked by our server logic (Unity Cloud Code).
  • Device and technical data — device model, operating system, language, app version and connection data (such as IP address) that our service providers need to deliver the Game.
  • Advertising data — only if you choose to watch an optional rewarded ad: our ad partners may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from IP address) and ad interaction data. See section 4.

The Game does not use a separate analytics or tracking SDK.

When you link an account (optional)

  • Google Play Games (Android): your Play Games player ID, so your progress can be restored on another device.
  • Sign in with Apple (iOS): an Apple user identifier. If you choose "Hide My Email", we never see your real email.
  • Username and password: the username you choose. Your password is handled by Unity Authentication; we never see it.

We do not receive your Google or Apple password.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store (through Unity IAP). We receive a receipt, product ID and transaction status so our server can verify the purchase and deliver the items. We never receive your payment card details.

Information you give us

  • Your in-game player name (names are checked by an automatic filter for offensive words).
  • Messages you send us for support or feedback, by email or on our Discord.

2. How we use information

  • To run the Game: create your account, save your progress and restore it on another device.
  • To run server-side game rules (summons, rewards, purchases) and to prevent cheating and fraud.
  • To deliver purchased items.
  • To show optional rewarded ads, only when you choose to watch one.
  • To fix bugs, answer support requests and meet legal obligations.

4. Advertising and your consent

The Game shows ads only when you choose to watch a rewarded ad (for example, to get extra energy). There are no forced or banner ads.

  • The first time you start the Game on a phone, it asks whether our ad partners may use data from your device to show ads that fit you better. If you choose Not now, ads are non-personalized and we ask partners not to sell your data.
  • You can change this any time in Settings → Other → Privacy choices.
  • You can also reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings (Android: Settings → Google → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

5. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the Game: Settings → Other → Account → Delete account, then type DELETE. This immediately deletes your server data (heroes, relics, currencies, cloud save) and your login. See how to delete your account.
  • Delete or get a copy by email: email augmentalvision@gmail.com and include the player ID shown on the Game's Settings → Other → Account page. We respond within 30 days.
  • Linking is optional: you can play as a guest without linking any account.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK or California), you may have additional rights, such as objecting to processing or complaining to your local data protection authority.

6. Data retention

We keep your account and progress for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account in the Game, your server data is deleted right away; email requests are completed within 30 days. Google and Apple keep their own purchase records under their policies. A copy of your progress may remain on your own device until you uninstall the Game.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and game currencies are protected on the server so they can't be changed by the app alone. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you where the law requires.

8. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, using safeguards described in their own policies.

9. Age

The Game is intended for players 18 and older. It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes (for example when multiplayer is added). We'll change the "Last updated" date above and, for significant changes, tell players in the Game or on our community channels.
