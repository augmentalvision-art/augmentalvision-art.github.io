Privacy Policy – Toon Cat Horror School

Effective October 7, 2026

This policy explains what information the Toon Cat Horror School app (Android package com.augmentalvision.cartooncatgame), made by Augmental Vision, operated by Dust In Peace LLC ("we"), collects and how it is used.

No accounts

The game has no sign-in and no in-game accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

Game data

Settings and progress are saved only on your device. We do not send them to our own servers.

Advertising (Unity Ads)

The game shows ads from Unity Ads, including optional rewarded ads (for example "Watch an ad to revive"). To show and measure ads, Unity may collect device information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model, operating system and how you interact with ads. This data is collected and handled by Unity under its own policy: Unity Privacy Policy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Apart from the ad provider described above, we do not share data with third parties.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
