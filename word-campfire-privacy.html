Word Campfire - Privacy Policy

Updated October 20, 2026 - Android and iOS

Who we are

Word Campfire is a Hmong Daw and English learning app operated by Dust In Peace LLC (YCrew Games). For privacy questions, access requests or deletion requests, email augmentalvision@gmail.com.

On your device

The app keeps course progress, practice history, settings and the version and time you accepted this notice. Sign-in session information may be cached so the app can reconnect to your save.

Speaking practice and microphone

Microphone audio is used only for short, on-device speaking checks. The app does not save audio clips and does not upload recordings. The microphone permission is requested only when you start speaking practice.

Cloud progress and accounts

After you accept the in-app notice, the app may create or reconnect a guest cloud session using Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Code and Cloud Save). Cloud saves include lesson progress, practice history and in-app XP/coins linked to a service player ID. You do not need a Unity account. If you link a sign-in provider (for example Google on Android, Sign in with Apple on iOS, or email), that provider verifies your sign-in and it is associated with your progress.

Advertising

Ads are shown only after the player chooses the adult (18 or older) age option and completes the advertising privacy check. The app uses Unity LevelPlay with ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Google AdMob. These services may process advertising and device identifiers, device and network information, approximate location derived from IP address and ad interactions to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and apply your choices. On iOS the app asks for App Tracking Transparency permission before ads start; if you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are not personalized using the advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, or from the app's advertising privacy settings. Rewarded "double coins" offers are verified by our server; an ad callback on the phone alone never grants coins.

Purchases and analytics

In-app purchases and optional analytics are currently turned off.

Notifications

Daily reminders are optional and can be turned off in Settings.

Children

Lessons are available to everyone. Advertising is limited to players who choose the adult age option; players who choose "Under 18" or keep ads off do not receive ads.

Keeping and deleting your data

Cloud progress is kept until you delete your account. You can delete your Word Campfire account and cloud progress from Settings in the app, or request deletion at the account deletion page or by email. You do not need to reinstall the app. Deleting your Word Campfire account does not delete your Google or Apple account. Advertising providers keep and delete data under their own policies.

Service providers

  • Unity game-player privacy policy (Unity Gaming Services, LevelPlay, ironSource Ads, Unity Ads)
  • Google privacy policy (AdMob, Google sign-in)
  • Apple privacy policy (Sign in with Apple, App Store)
