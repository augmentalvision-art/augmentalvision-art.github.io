Loudhead Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026 · Developer: YCrew Games, a brand of Dust In Peace LLC

This policy explains what information the Loudhead mobile game collects, why, and the choices you have. Loudhead is a horror game for players aged 13 and older.

What stays on your device

Your progress and settings are saved only on your device. The game has no accounts and we do not receive this data. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Information collected by our partners

WhatWho and why
Advertising ID, device and app info, approximate location (from IP address), ad interactions, diagnosticsUnity Ads, to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them, prevent fraud and (where you allow it) personalize them

Partner policy: Unity. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not collect contacts, photos, precise location, microphone or camera data.

Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone's settings.
  • Uninstalling the game removes all game data from your device.

Children

Loudhead is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data and to object to certain processing. Because the game has no accounts, most of what we could identify is held by our ad and store partners; email us and we will help with any request within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
