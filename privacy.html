Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026. This policy explains what information the Left 2 Die app (Android package com.PlanZ.Left2Die) and its iPhone/iPad version, made by YCrew Games, a brand of Dust In Peace LLC ("we"), collects and how it is used.

Your account

When you start the game you can Sign in with Google (Android), Sign in with Apple (iPhone/iPad) or Play as Guest. If you sign in with Apple, we receive an Apple account identifier and, only if you choose to share them, your name and email (which may be an Apple private relay address).

If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account's email address, name and a Google account identifier. We use them only to create and recognise your game account so your progress can be restored on any phone. We do not receive your Google password.

Guest accounts use a random player ID and are kept only on that phone.

Game progress (cloud save)

Your game progress (in-game cash, revive tokens, unlocked skins and pets, campaign progress, settings such as language, and whether you bought Remove Ads) is stored with Unity Gaming Services (Unity Authentication and Cloud Save) under your player ID, so it can be restored after a reinstall or on a new phone.

Online play

Online co-op and versus use Unity Relay and Lobby to connect players. The name you enter and in-game actions are shared with the other players in your session.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), Unity Ads and Google AdMob. These providers may collect device identifiers (such as the Android advertising ID), IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to show, measure and personalise ads and to prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings. Their policies: Unity, ironSource, Google.

Your choices about ads

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent (using Google's consent tool) before ads use your data for personalised ads. You can change your choice any time from ACCOUNT > AD PRIVACY in the game. On iPhone/iPad, the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency); you can change this in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation of what was bought so we can deliver it.

Diagnostics

If the app crashes or hits an error, Unity may collect technical information such as device model, operating system version, app version and the error details, to help us fix problems.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the service providers above, to run the game, or if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account and progress while your account exists. You can delete your account and all saved progress at any time in the game (ACCOUNT → DELETE ACCOUNT) or by following the steps on our Delete Account page.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under the GDPR, or US states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal data, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to withdraw consent. We use your data to provide the game you asked for (account and progress), for our legitimate interest in keeping the game working and secure (diagnostics), and with your consent where the law requires it (personalised ads). Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. To use your rights, delete your account in the game or email us; you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Left 2 Die is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored by our providers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.

Terms

Use of the game is also covered by our Terms of Service.
