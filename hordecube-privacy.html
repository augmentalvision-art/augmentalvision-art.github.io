Hordecube Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026 · App: Hordecube (com.ycrewvision.hordecube) · Developer: YCrew Games, a brand of Dust In Peace LLC

This policy explains what information Hordecube collects, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. Hordecube is intended for players aged 13 and older.

Information we collect

WhatWhy
Player account ID – a random ID created by Unity Authentication when you first play (guest account)To save your progress, run your wallet and purchases on our server, and let you play online
Sign-in details if you choose to sign in: Google Play Games ID and display name, Sign in with Apple ID, or the email address of your Unity accountTo back up your progress and let you play on other devices
Game progress: coins, gems, skins, relics, missions, season pass, settings, best scoresTo run the game and keep your progress safe
Player name you type for online playShown to other players in online matches. Names are filtered for offensive words.
Purchase records: product, Google Play / App Store order ID and purchase tokenTo verify purchases, deliver gems or Remove Ads, and prevent fraud (each order is paid out only once)
Device and usage data: device model, OS version, language, app version, approximate location (country/region from IP address), gameplay events, crash reportsAnalytics and crash reporting to fix bugs and improve the game
Advertising ID and ad interaction dataTo show ads, cap how often you see them, measure them, and (if you allow it) personalize them

We do not collect your contacts, photos, precise GPS location, or microphone/camera data. Reminder notifications are created on your device; we do not send them from a server.

Third-party services

Hordecube uses these services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Analytics, Multiplayer, Cloud Diagnostics, Unity Ads, LevelPlay mediation) – Unity privacy policy
  • Google (Google Play Games sign-in, Google Play Billing, AdMob ads, User Messaging Platform consent) – Google privacy policy, how Google uses data from partner apps
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple, App Store purchases) on iPhone – Apple privacy policy

We do not sell your personal information. Ad partners may use the advertising ID to show personalized ads only where you have allowed it.

Your choices

  • Ad personalization: in the EEA, UK and other regions where it applies, a consent form asks you on first launch; change it any time in Settings › AD PRIVACY CHOICES. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. On iPhone, the app asks permission before tracking.
  • Analytics: in regions that require consent, analytics only starts after you agree in the consent form.
  • Notifications: off unless you turn them on; switch them off in Settings › NOTIFICATIONS or in your phone settings.
  • Delete your account: Settings › DELETE ACCOUNT in the game, or see how to delete your account.
  • Access or correction: email us and we will help.

How long we keep data

Account and progress data are kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete your account. Purchase order records are kept for fraud prevention and accounting as required by law. Analytics and crash data are kept in aggregated or limited form according to Unity's retention periods.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Your coins, gems and items are stored on the server where they cannot be edited from the phone.

Children

Hordecube is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will delete the data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to or limit certain processing. Email us to make a request; we respond within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy. Significant changes will be shown in the game. The date at the top shows the latest version.
