Dead Leveling Terms · Dead Leveling Privacy

Request account deletion

YCrew Games is operated by Dust In Peace LLC. You can request deletion of your game account and associated data by email, even if you no longer have the game installed.

Start your request

In Dead Leveling, find your player ID under Settings → Account & Cloud → Delete Account. You can leave this blank if you cannot access the game.

Never include your password, verification codes or payment card details. These fields stay in this page until you choose to open an email draft; this page does not send or upload them.

Review and send the draft in your email app. Opening a draft does not submit a request or delete an account.

If the button does not open an email app, email augmentalvision@gmail.com with the game name and your player ID or sign-in email.

What happens next

Support will verify account ownership and confirm the deletion steps and expected completion time. Where possible, send the request from the email linked to your game account. You do not need to reinstall the game to make a request.

What your request covers

Request removal of your game account, linked sign-in identifiers and associated saved progress, including inventory and game currencies. If any records must be retained, ask support to explain which records, why, and for how long.

This request does not delete your Google, Apple or email account. Those providers manage their own accounts and purchase records.

From Dead Leveling

Open Settings → Account & Cloud → Delete Account → Email Deletion Request. Review and send the email to support. This is a deletion request, not an instant account wipe.
