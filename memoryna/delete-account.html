Delete your account

Fabled Oath: Second Dawn · YCrew Games (Dust In Peace LLC)

Delete it in the Game (instant)

  1. Open Fabled Oath: Second Dawn.
  2. Tap the Settings gear (top right) → Other → Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm.

You need an internet connection so the server copy can be deleted too.

Or ask us by email

If you can't open the Game, email augmentalvision@gmail.com with the subject "Fabled Oath account deletion". Include your player ID (shown on the Account page) or the username you linked. We complete requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your player account and any linked logins (Google Play Games, Sign in with Apple, username).
  • Your heroes, relics, gear, gems, coins, energy and story progress stored on our servers.
  • Your cloud save.

What is kept

  • Purchase records kept by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own policies. Deleting your account does not refund purchases.
  • Progress saved on your own device stays there until you uninstall the Game.

Deletion can't be undone. See our Privacy Policy for details.
